Local data and your control

Cases, preferences and checklist progress are stored locally with AsyncStorage, which is not encrypted. Do not enter patient identifiers. Device backups and exported files may retain data. Source links open external websites. No account or cloud sync is implemented. Native SDK, network and backup behavior need release-specific verification; zero transmission or PHI compliance is not asserted. Export and recovery controls are available in Preferences & data.
